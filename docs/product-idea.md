# PawQuest Puzzle Ball — Product Documentation

## 1. Product Overview

**Name:** PawQuest Puzzle Ball
**Category:** Dog enrichment toy / slow-feeder
**One-liner:** A durable, refillable rubber ball that turns mealtime into 15–30 minutes of active, rewarding play.

The PawQuest Puzzle Ball is a reusable enrichment toy for dogs. Owners twist it open, fill the inner chamber with kibble or treats, choose a difficulty setting, and lock it shut. As the dog rolls, nudges, and paws the ball, food drops out through adjustable openings. The dog stays happily busy until the chamber is empty — then the owner refills it and the play starts again.

Unlike disposable toys, this is designed to be reused for years: it is refillable, washable, and chew-resistant.

---

## 2. The Problem

- **Boredom:** Dogs left alone or between walks get bored and destructive.
- **Fast eating:** Many dogs gulp meals in seconds, which can cause bloating, vomiting, and poor digestion.
- **Short-lived toys:** Most toys are interesting for minutes, then ignored.
- **Disposable products:** Cheap toys break quickly and end up in landfill.

## 3. The Solution

One reusable toy that solves all four:

- **Engages the dog** through a food-reward puzzle that rewards effort.
- **Slows eating** by making the dog work for every piece of food.
- **Lasts** because it is made from chew-resistant natural rubber and is refillable.
- **Is reusable** — empty, rinse, refill, repeat, for years.

---

## 4. How It Works

1. **Twist open** the cap at the top of the ball.
2. **Fill** the inner chamber with kibble or treats (holds about 1–2 cups).
3. **Set the difficulty** using the sliding disc — small, medium, or large openings.
4. **Lock** the cap and roll the ball to your dog.
5. The dog **rolls and paws** the ball; food drops out through the openings as a reward.
6. When empty, **refill and repeat**.
